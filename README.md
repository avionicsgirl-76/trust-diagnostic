# What's Actually Holding Your Group Together?

**The Structural Trust Diagnostic — from the Grey Cell Protocols**

Live at: *https://trust-diagnostic.onrender.com*

## What this is

A free, twelve-question self-assessment of whether a group runs on **structural trust** — trust created by design, verification, and accountability — or on **emotional trust**: familiarity, affection, camaraderie, and maybe a little luck.

It takes about three minutes. It scores six domains of how a group actually operates:

- **Access** — who can touch what, and why
- **Verification** — whether anything gets checked, or checking would feel like an insult
- **Reconstructability** — whether decisions leave a trail anyone could examine
- **Corrective feedback** — what happens when someone says the leader is wrong
- **Entry and removal** — how people get in, and whether there's any way out
- **The load-bearing person** — what would catch your most trusted member if you were wrong about them

The results name what each weak domain predicts, in plain terms.

## Who it's for

Anyone who belongs to a group they'd hate to watch fail: a team, a church, a workplace, a crew, a preparedness network, a political or activist organization. You need one real group in mind and the willingness to answer for how it actually works in practice rather than how it's supposed to.

## Why I wrote it

Groups that believe they have a trust problem usually have a liking problem. They like the people they are working with, and that leads to emotional trust. The issue is that groups usually fail due to internal factors that no one noticed, and everyone thought they had handled. This instrument exists to make those weaknesses nameable early, while they're still just drift.

The framework underneath it is the Grey Cell Protocols, which is my counterintelligence-derived doctrine for detecting and resisting internal degradation in groups and organizations. 

## What it does — and doesn't — tell you

This is a self-report instrument. It measures how your group's trust is *built*, not whether anyone in it is trustworthy. A weak domain does not mean misconduct is occurring. It means that in that domain, if something went wrong, your group would find out late or not at all.

Your answers are scored entirely in your browser. They are never transmitted, stored, or seen by anyone, including me. No email is required to see your results, and I do not collect your data.
