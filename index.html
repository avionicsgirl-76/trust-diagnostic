What this does and doesn't tell you

This is a self-report instrument. It measures how your group's trust is built, not whether anyone in it is trustworthy. A weak domain does not mean misconduct is occurring or that anyone intends harm — most structural weakness is drift, not attack. What it does mean: in that domain, if something went wrong, your group would find out late or not at all. Structural trust is not suspicion of everyone; it's the design that makes appropriate behavior more likely, misconduct more detectable, and failure more recoverable — so that your relationships don't have to do a job they were never built for.

The fix for every weak domain above is the same discipline: installing structural trust without turning your group cold — the components, the sequence, and how to do it without triggering the exact defensiveness you're worried about.
